Provenance & Care

How is the fiber sourced sustainably?

We partner with local Andean communities using the traditional 'Chaccu' method. This centuries-old communal shearing ritual ensures the vicuñas are safely captured, shorn, and released back into the wild unharmed.

What makes Raw Vicuña so expensive?

Vicuñas produce only about 500 grams of fiber every three years. The scarcity, combined with the extreme difficulty of harvesting such fine fiber, makes it the most precious textile in the world.

Is there a warranty for this investment?

Every Heirloom Overcoat comes with a lifetime repair guarantee. Should the garment require re-shaping or minor repair due to wear, our artisans will restore it to its original glory.
